## Eighth session update — legal rule verification against real Tribunals Ontario sources

This is the pass Ebin asked to be deferred to "tomorrow" back on the first night. Method: fetched the actual current official PDF forms directly from tribunalsontario.ca (not blog summaries) for N1, N4, N5, N8, N12, N13, read them directly, and corrected the app's rule values against what they actually say. **All six official PDFs are saved in the repo at `src/lib/ltb/pdf/official-forms/`** for future reference/field-mapping work.

**What was confirmed correct** (no change needed): N4's 14/7-day notice periods, N5's 20/14-day notice periods and 7-day cure period, N1's 90-day notice and 12-month spacing, N8's 60/28-day notice periods, N12's 60-day notice period and one-month's-rent-or-alternate-unit compensation.

**Real errors found and fixed:**
- **N1 guideline was wrong**: had 2.5% (last year's figure), corrected to **2.1%** — Ontario's actual published 2026 guideline. This directly affects the "exceeds guideline" warning shown to landlords.
- **N12 and N13 both had a fabricated daily/weekly exception**: I'd assumed (without checking) that these forms shorten the notice period for daily/weekly tenancies, the same way N4/N5/N8 do. The official forms don't — N12 is a flat 60 days and N13 a flat 120 days regardless of rent frequency. Fixed both.
- **N13 compensation was completely unmodeled** (just a free-text field) despite being one of the most consequential parts of this notice. Built a real calculator (`calculateN13Compensation` in `rules/n13.ts`) implementing the official form's actual rules: 3 months' rent (≥5 units) or 1 month's rent (<5 units) for demolition/conversion; a "lesser of the cap or the actual repair period" rule when the tenant plans to move back in after a repair/renovation; a mobile-home/land-lease exception (lesser of one year's rent or $3,000); and a full exemption when the landlord was ordered to demolish/repair by law. Added the two new toggles this needed to `n13-new.tsx`, wired the computed compensation into validation and the generated PDF.
- **N13 was also missing the mobile-home/land-lease notice-period exception** (1 year instead of 120 days) — added.
- **N5 was missing a real rule**: "A landlord cannot give a second N5 unless at least 7 days have passed since the first." Added as a BLOCKER in `n5Workflow.ts`.
- **N4 and N8's "biweekly" categorization was an unverified assumption** — neither official form actually names "biweekly" as its own category; only "daily or weekly" get the shorter period. Reclassified biweekly to the general (longer) period for both, the more conservative reading.
- **N8's real gap, now documented rather than silently wrong**: the official form has **5 distinct grounds** (persistent late payment, no longer qualifies for public/subsidized housing, employment-conditioned unit ended, terminated Agreement of Purchase and Sale for a proposed condo, rehab/therapeutic tenancy period ended) — this system only implements the first one. Not fixed this pass (bigger UI change, see Next Up), but now explicitly flagged in the code and in validation instead of silently pretending persistent-late-payment is the only ground.

**What's still genuinely unverified, and why `needsReview: true` stays on most of these**: the service-method extra-day figures (mail = +5 days, courier/hand-to-unit = +1 day, etc., in `serviceMethodRules.ts`) were not checked this pass — the official forms reference a separate "Instructions" document for this that wasn't fetched. Every notice type's date calculation still depends on these, so `needsReview` stays `true` at the rule level even where the notice-period figure itself is now confirmed. Only N1 is fully `needsReview: false`, since its calculation never touches service-method timing at all.

**A discrepancy I deliberately did NOT "fix"**: multiple secondary sources (law firm blogs, aggregators) claimed Bill 60 (passed by the Ontario legislature Nov 24, 2025) already reduced the N4 notice period from 14 to 7 days for monthly/yearly tenancies. The actual live official N4 form, fetched today, still says 14 days. I trusted the primary source over the secondary commentary and left N4 at 14 days, with a comment in `rules/n4.ts` flagging this as a watch item — the bill may not have been proclaimed into force yet. Worth a periodic re-check, not something to silently override based on a blog post.

**The edge function was redeployed (version 3)** with all the same corrections, since its server-side rule copies (used by `record_actual_service`) would otherwise have drifted out of sync with these client-side fixes — exactly the kind of bug this session already caught once before with N1/N8.
